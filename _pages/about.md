I'm a Master of Computing (Computer Science Specialisation) student at the **National University of Singapore (NUS)**. My current work focuses on **data management**, **semantic query processing**, and **systems**.

Broadly, I am interested in:

- Data management systems
- Semantic query processing
- Query and retrieval over structured and unstructured data
- Systems for data-intensive applications

During my undergraduate studies at CUHK-Shenzhen, I was advised by [Prof. Chenhao Ma](https://chenhao-ma.github.io/). At NUS, I am advised by [Prof. Wenqi Jiang](https://wenqijiang.github.io/).

My work includes **KATS**, an end-to-end task-oriented dataset search system that builds a dynamically updatable knowledge graph from scientific literature and introduces the CS-TDS benchmark, published in PVLDB (VLDB 2026). Earlier, I developed **SemExplorer**, a semantic search interface that helps researchers find datasets using free-form natural language queries together with structural constraints, presented as a demo at ACM SIGMOD 2025.

I also enjoy teaching and mentoring. During my undergraduate studies, I served as a teaching assistant for Database Systems, Design and Analysis of Algorithms, Probability & Statistics, and introductory computer science and Java courses, and received a TA/USTF Award for my teaching contributions.
